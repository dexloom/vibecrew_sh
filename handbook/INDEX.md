## How to use this handbook

These pages describe **how VibeCrew works**. They do not describe **your**
install — what projects you have, what is running right now, what your config
says. For that, look at the live state ([10-live-state.md](10-live-state.md)).

If a page does not cover something, say so. An honest "the handbook doesn't
cover that" is worth more than a confident guess.
